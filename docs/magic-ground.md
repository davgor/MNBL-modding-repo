# Magic ground overlay (MMR-260) and live mutation (MMR-261), 2026-10-20

Magic-mutated ground is drawn by one runtime decal overlay, and live mutations also retype terrain. This is MMR-249's delivery A: no school terrain layers. Generated magic variants are visual only; only live mutation cells (MMR-261's) change a face type, a battle class or a colour grade (RT-20, RT-26). Research: `docs/research/map-epic-2-findings.md` sections 6, 7 and 13. Textures and atlas: `docs/magic-ground-textures.md`.

**MMR-291 (2026-10-21): the veins are drawn as overlay meshes.** acc5 and the decal lab showed the campaign map draws scene decals only from Native's prebaked decal atlas, so these decals (module art) draw nothing. With `roads.json overlayMeshes.veins.mode` `mesh` (the default) every drawn land stamp below is drawn as a terrain-draped mesh piece with its atlas cell instead, and the overlay installs no land decals (`MagicGroundRuntime` syncs an empty plan; `veinsAsMeshes` in its record); mode `decal` brings the decals (MMR-247 D's tinted vanilla fallbacks) back. The plan, the graph, the reveal and the hooks below are unchanged. See `docs/overlay-meshes.md`.

Code: `World/MagicGround.cs` (pure: source, snapshot, face types, battle classes, grade cells, the decal plan and its installer), `World/MagicVeins.cs` (pure: the vein graph, MMR-288), `World/MagicGroundRuntime.cs` (engine side), `GeneratedMapScene` (the terrain-type members). Policy: `roads.json` `magic`, `terrain-materials.json` `mutation`. Tests: `tools/Test-MagicGround.ps1 -BundleA -BundleB [-Dump DIR]` (`tests/World/MagicGroundTests.cs`); preview `tools/render_magic_veins.py`, evidence `evidence/world/magic-ground.json`. Live mutation (MMR-261, below): `World/WorldMutations.cs`, `Campaign/WorldMutationBehavior.cs`, policy `worldgen/policies/mutation.json`, `worldgen/producer-archive.json`; tests `tools/Test-WorldMutation.ps1 -BundleA [-Producer fwg.exe]` (`tests/World/WorldMutationTests.cs`), evidence `evidence/world/world-mutation.json`.

## Source

From the bundle's `source/world.json`, read in the climate's streaming pass (`GeneratedClimateSystem.ReadSource`). Every part is optional, so a magic-off world loads with no magic ground and refuses live mutations.
- `layers.biome_variant`: -1, or an index into `terrain.magical_biomes` (its `core`, `magic_school` and `color`). The catalogue holds 156 entries: 13 cores, the 8 visible schools of each first, then the 4 hidden ones. **`biome_variant / 12` is not the core**; the overlay reads the catalogue entry.
- `layers.ley_<school>`: the potency of the variant's school at the sample.
- `magic.enabled`, `mutation_threshold`, `dominance_margin`, `school_order`.

## Mapping

- A source sample (row r from the north pole line, column c, n = gridSide - 1 per period) is the logical point (W frac(c / n - meridian / 360), H r / n): `projection.json` forward.sourceSample, as the ruin markers use it.
- A physical point's sample is its nearest node (inverse.sourceGrid, as `PeriodicWorldCoordinates.SourceUid`): the column modulo the period, so every strip image and both sides of the seam reach one sample; a point past a pole line clamps to the pole node. Column n duplicates column 0.
- Pole rows are lines, not ground: they carry no decal and retype nothing.
- Every other sample lies in exactly one travel cell, the cell holding its point. A 6.25-unit travel cell holds about two samples (source cells are 6.25 x 3.125). Default taken: nearest-node rounding at a travel cell's centre would tie on half the columns and drop them, so the partition is by sample point.

## Snapshot

`WorldSurfaceOverlay` holds one immutable `MagicSnapshot`: the generated variants plus the live cells (by sample) and nodes. A change builds a whole new snapshot and swaps the reference with `Interlocked.CompareExchange`, so a reader on any thread sees one whole state.
- `Replace(cells, nodes)`: the whole live state (a load's replay of the stored cells).
- `Apply(cells, nodes)`: a cast's changed cells merged in; nodes added. A cell with variant -1 clears its sample (no magic ground there, whatever was generated).
- `Disable()`: RT-37. Live cells and nodes are dropped and later casts refused; generated magic ground stays drawn.
- Each change returns a `MagicChange`: the travel cells whose type changed, the changed samples, and the land shares by type before and after (RT-20).

## Terrain type (live cells only)

- A land travel cell holding live samples takes `MutatedType(school, core)` of its samples (terrain-materials.json `mutation`: hidden schools take their look school's, `coreOverrides` win, so water on cold tundra, land ice and boreal forest gives Snow). With samples of two types, the type most give wins; a tie keeps the first in row-major order (default taken).
- Only types in `mutation.landValid` (Plain, Desert, Snow, Forest, Steppe, Swamp; never Dune). Water cores (ocean, lake), unknown schools and non-land cells (mountain, sea, lake, river, decks) are never retyped, so passability never changes.
- **Faces.** `MagicFaces.Build` gives each navmesh face whose group is a land face type its centre's cell's type: every strip image's twin, since the physical column wraps to its logical cell. Bridge, mountain and water faces never change. The engine's faces are enumerated at the map load (`Scene.GetNavMeshFaceCount`, `GetNavMeshPathFaceRecord`, `GetNavMeshCenterPosition`), only when some cell is retyped.
- **Queries.** `GeneratedMapScene` re-implements `IMapScene`, and its `GetFaceTerrainType`, `GetTerrainTypeAtPosition`, `GetEnvironmentTerrainTypes` and `GetEnvironmentTerrainTypesCount` answer from the face table (a `volatile byte[]` per face index, 0 for the face's own group). The two environment queries copy the installed walk exactly, including its cumulative probe point. `GetFaceTerrainType` allocates nothing. Every managed caller asks through `IMapScene` (party speed, spotting, tracks, perks, auto-resolve power, battle and conversation scene choice, incidents); native pathfinding and `MapEvent._eventTerrainType` read the face group and do not follow the overlay (findings section 7).
- **Load order.** `GeneratedMapScene.Load` builds the snapshot (with MMR-261's restored cells) and the face table after the scene's read, before the map counts as loaded. `Campaign.LoadMapScene` (stage 2 of `DoLoadingForGameType`) runs before `OnGameLoaded`, which calls `MobileParty.PreAfterLoad` and `ComputePathAfterLoad` (installed IL). MMR-261's `SyncData` is read by `LoadBehaviorData` in stage 0, before both.

## Battle class (RT-26)

The battle map's patch is registered in `GeneratedBattleTerrain.CellPatches[scene]` at the map load, before `AfterLoad` installs the map. A battle cell (4 units, canonical column) whose centre lies in a retyped cell takes `CellSamples.ClassOverride = mission-scenes.json faceClass[remapped type]`; the resolver then chooses its scene as for any cell of that class. On a live change, `GeneratedBattleTerrain.Repatch` re-chooses exactly the battle cells of the changed travel cells with MMR-283's `RebuildCells`, which writes every strip image of each into the installed byte array.

## Decals: veins (MMR-288)

Magic ground is drawn as veins fanning out from their sources, like spreading corruption, not as one round stamp per sample (the owner's request after acc4). `World/MagicVeins.cs` builds a deterministic vein graph per magic zone; `MagicGround.Plan` draws its land stamps as entity-less scene decals (MMR-249's technique through the map dressing's engine target: `Decal.CreateDecal(null)`, `SetMaterial`, `AddDecalInstance(d, "editor_set", true)`, `SetIsVisible`, `SetVectorArgument`, `Frame`, half depth max(3, footprint reach / 0.85) as the water stamps, docs/map-dressing.md, MMR-262; `RemoveDecalInstance` only on the live scene: the map's teardown runs after `Scene.ClearAll` took them, so it drops the handles with no engine call). Policy `roads.json magic`; the atlas contract is map2-rev5 C1 (`docs/magic-ground-textures.md`).

**Graph** (`MagicVeins.Build(snapshot, policy)`, pure managed, no unseeded choice, every loop in canonical order, canonical physical frame):
- **Zones**: 8-connected source samples (pole rows excluded, columns wrapping) of one look school (a hidden school takes `mutation.hiddenLooks`: blood infernal, void umbral, rot water, eldritch weave) and one origin. Generated zones are built from the generated variants alone, so their graph is the same whatever is cast: a live cell over a generated node hides it (`VeinNode.Hidden`; its stamps are not made) and every other generated stamp keeps its id and decal. Live zones are the live cells' own. Water samples (ocean and lake cores, samples on water travel cells) belong to the zones too, so the graph crosses the shore: each node carries `OnWater`.
- **Sources**: a generated zone's potency maxima (its highest sample, then each local maximum at least `veins.sourceSpacing` units from the chosen ones). A live zone's sources are the cast nodes of its school (`MagicNode`, the capital): at the node when it lies within `spacing` of the zone, else at the zone sample nearest the node, whose Order then starts at that distance in growth steps. A live zone with no node of its school falls back to the potency rule (default taken).
- **Growth** (space colonisation, default taken): every zone sample is an attraction point weighted 0.5 + 0.5 x its potency above the threshold; each round every point pulls its nearest node within 3 x `spacing`, and each pulled node grows one step (0.75 x `stampLength`) toward the weighted mean direction (toward its nearest point when the pulls cancel). A step must stay in the zone (its nearest sample is a zone sample) and 0.45 steps from every node; a point within `spacing / 2` of a node is reached. Branches so run out from the sources down the potency gradient, about `spacing` apart, and fork where the points split. Growth ends when no node grows.
- **Smoothing** (after the first re-render with the vein art showed zigzags; defaults taken): a node's first child adds its parent edge's heading to the pull (momentum 1) and turns at most 45 degrees from it; a later child (a branch) leaves at most 75 degrees off it. A source grows along the raw pull. A step that cannot be placed grows nothing (a raw fallback brought the zigzags back); the zone's other nodes still reach its points (coverage below).
- **Width** (gaps fix, defaults taken): the smallest of three caps, clamped to [`minWidth`, `width`], then no child wider than its parent:
  - a pipe model in which each node adds its own flow: s^g = `minWidth`^g + the sum of its children's s^g, g = `forkExponent` (a vein thickens with what it feeds, an unbranched one too; the plain pipe left 61-67% of land nodes at `minWidth` from just past the source to the edge);
  - a taper along the node's longest path: `minWidth` + (`width` - `minWidth`) x h / (h + d), h its height above its farthest tip, d its depth below its source;
  - an edge cap: `minWidth` + (`width` - `minWidth`) x Edge(depth), where depth is the node's sample's 8-connected steps inside its zone (1 on the rim) and Edge(depth) = clamp((depth - 0.5) / `veins.edgeSamples`, 0, 1) (sources take 1).
- **Alpha** (gaps fix): the smaller of a potency fade (`veins.minAlpha` at the mutation threshold, smoothstep to 1 over `veins.fadeBand` of potency above it) and an edge fade `minAlpha` + (1 - `minAlpha`) x Edge(depth); sources 1. A stamp takes its owner node's alpha; the decal carries it as `SetFactor1(alpha << 24 | 0xFFFFFF)` (white, so only the alpha changes; a full-alpha stamp keeps no tint). Static evidence: the terrain's tiled decal path multiplies the decal sample by `factor_color_1`, alpha included, before the alpha blend (`Shaders/Sources/pbr_shading_functions.rsh:278`, also `gbuffer_functions.rsh:263`); not seen in game.
- **Order**: graph distance (edges) from the node's source.

**Stamps** (atlas columns trunk 0, fork 1, tip 2, burst 3; the stamp's u runs along the growth, source side at u = 0):
- **burst** at each source: a square box `burstScale` x the source's width, yaw along its widest child's edge (so one ray leads into the source's main vein; a source with no child keeps a hashed yaw);
- **trunk** on each edge into a node with children: centred on the edge, yaw along it, box `stampLength` x the child's width (stamps 0.75 x L apart overlap by a quarter);
- **tip** on each edge into a tip node: its end (u = 0.85) at the tip, box `stampLength` x the parent's width (the tip tapers in the art);
- **fork** where it fits (`MagicVeins.ForkFits`): a non-source node with two or more children whose two widest open at most 40 degrees each side of their bisector and whose parent edge comes in within 40 degrees of it. Its split (u = 0.4) sits at the node, yaw the bisector, box width the node's width and length `min(stampLength, 0.4 W / tan(half angle))`: the art's children leave at dv/du +-0.4, so the fitted box opens them at the graph children's angle. Elsewhere the trunks and tips meet at the node on their own. (A fork at every branching node, at full length, put its prongs beside the real children and read as a star of four or five spokes in the first re-render.)
**Drawn components** (`MagicGround.Plan(snapshot, policy, waterDrawn)`, gaps fix): the land stamps (plus the graph's water stamps when a vein layer draws them) are joined to the stamp upstream of each (a trunk or tip to its parent node's trunk or the source's burst, a fork to the trunk into its node). Each component is a tree with one root:
- a burst: drawn when the component holds at least `veins.minStamps` (4) stamps, so no lone burst or burst with one or two stamps (the scattered small stars of the first preview);
- a stamp whose upstream is a water stamp no layer draws (a land branch hanging off a water node): re-rooted at a burst on its first land node (id `<zone>.<node>.shore`) only with at least 2 x `minStamps` land stamps and no drawn burst within `sourceSpacing / 2` (largest first; a live cast's fragment needs only `minStamps`, anywhere), else not drawn. Without the spacing rule B' drew 67 shore stars, 51 of them within 24 units of another burst;
- a stamp whose upstream node a live cell hides: drawn with at least `minStamps` land stamps (it runs out of the cast's ground), else not drawn.
`MagicGroundPlan.Dropped` names the graph stamps of components not drawn (water ones too when `waterDrawn`), `Components` counts them. Whether water is drawn comes from the layers: a layer that implements `IMagicWaterVeins` with `DrawsWaterStamps` true (MMR-290's magic water must add it at integration; until then the ground plans as if no water is drawn).
- **Atlas** `(0.25, 0.25, column / 4, row / 4)`: the row is the owner node's core group (arid desert; verdant grassland, forest, rainforest, marsh; rock exposed_rock; cold boreal_forest, cold_tundra, land_ice, tundra, snow); `flipV` uses `(3 - row) / 4`, `flipU` turns every stamp by 180 degrees (the art is symmetric about v = 0.5, so that flips u only). Both are off until MMR-247's screenshots settle them.
- **Material** `mnbl_magic_<look>`; `Material.GetFromResource` null draws nothing: the installer counts it, records the material once and never throws.
- **Fallback** (MMR-247 D, acc5): the campaign map draws scene decals from Native's prebaked decal atlas only, and no module texture has a rect in it (docs/map-dressing.md "Decal atlas"), so the 775 vein decals of acc5 drew nothing. Every stamp carries a vanilla fallback (`roads.json magic.fallback`), which the engine target draws while its material's diffuse texture has no rect: a burst the village stamp (`mainmap_decal_undervillage`, box as the stamp's), any other kind the khuzait path stroke (`decal_khuzait_path_a`, its right stroke: about 0.22 of its cell across, 0.9 along v), turned a quarter so it runs along the vein, the box `across` (4.5) x the width and `along` (1.1) x the length; tint (`SetFactor1`) per channel 255 x the linear school colour (`fallback.colours`) / the linear core of the stamp (`core`, measured in the atlas), clamped at 255, alpha the stamp's. A multiply cannot lighten, so the colours are the vein art's core colours with weave and air deepened. The look is unverified until the decal lab (docs/map-epic-2-acceptance.md "Decal lab") or the next run.
- **Water**: a stamp whose owner node or centre is on water (a water core, a water travel cell) is not drawn by the ground overlay; MMR-290 draws those (`VeinStamp.OnWater`). The ground overlay's land stamps never lie on a water node or a water cell. MMR-247 (acc5 c13: a tip centred on sea ground on A', two sea and three lake centres on B'): the split was by graph node and travel cell, but a land cell's edge can dip under the sea or a lake plane, so a stamp whose centre a shown water plane covers is a water stamp too (`MagicVeins.ShowsWater`: the lower of the encoded and the collision height, `MapDressing.LowGroundOf`, under the datum or inside a shown lake plane's box under its plane; `MagicGroundSource.Lakes` are the map dressing's planes, passed in by `WorldReconstruction`, else computed by the same rule). A shore burst is never re-rooted at such a node either. Test-MagicGround recomputes it: 0 drawn land stamps on shown water on A' and B', with and without the water layer and with the Nerdun cast (1 and 5 graph stamps move to the water layer by the plane test alone).
- **Cap** `maxDecals` 2500 land stamps per world (was 6000, above the whole 5,000 budget, so it could never protect it); past it generated stamps go outermost first (highest Order, a fork before the trunk into its node, then the thinnest; live stamps and bursts stay), so every kept stamp stays joined to its burst (tested by thinning to half). Neither world reaches it (B' draws 1,443; 2,012 with a water layer).
- **Seams**: stamps are in the canonical frame; each decal is drawn at its periodic image nearest the camera and moved on a camera wrap (`PeriodicMapCameraView` calls `MagicGroundRuntime.RefreshImages`). The installer syncs by stamp id (`G|L<zone's lowest sample>.<node>.<kind>`), so a live change or a reveal step redraws only the stamps of the zones it touches.

**Live reveal.** A cast's veins grow outward from the capital over its delta's cells: a live node of Order k appears at `Since + revealHours x k / (the cast's highest Order)`. `Since` is the cast node's key point's first batch day x 24 (campaign hours), set by `MutationOperations.Grown` from MMR-261's edit log both when the delta is applied and when a save loads, so the stored mutation document is enough to rebuild the reveal. `revealHours` is 48 (default taken: two in-game days). `WorldMutationBehavior`'s campaign tick (engine thread, never during a load) calls `MagicGroundRuntime.Advance(CampaignTime.Now.ToHours)`, which costs one comparison until the next stamp is due and then redraws only the newly revealed stamps. A load draws what its campaign time shows, so a Continue after the time has passed shows the veins fully grown. The clock is `MagicRevealClock` (pure, in `MagicGround.cs`, tested with the recording target): `Start` at install, `Restart` at a live change, `Tick` on the campaign tick. With no campaign clock at install (`Campaign.Current` null or `CampaignTime` unreadable: +infinity) every vein shows grown, the runtime logs `magic_ground_warning:no campaign clock at install`, and the first tick with a real time re-hides what that time does not show yet. Known limit: a console cast in mid-day starts its reveal at the start of that day (the edit log keeps whole days), so part of its growth shows at once. Generated veins are always shown.

## API for MMR-289 and MMR-290

- `MagicVeins.Build(MagicSnapshot, RoadsPolicy.MagicPolicy)` -> `MagicVeinGraph`; the loaded map's graph is `MagicGroundRuntime.Veins` (fully grown) with the reveal clock `MagicGroundRuntime.Hours`; `MagicGround.Plan(...).Graph` offline.
- `MagicVeinGraph`: `Zones` (`VeinZone` {Index, Key, Look, Live, Samples, FirstNode, NodeCount, Sources, MaxPotency}), `Sources` (`VeinSource` {Node, Zone, Cast (`MagicNode` or null), Since, OrderOffset}), `Nodes` (`VeinNode` {Index, Zone, Source, Parent (-1 at a source), Children, X, Y (canonical physical), Width, Order, Sample, Variant, School, Look, Core, CoreRow, Potency, OnWater, Live, IsSource, RevealAt, Alpha, Hidden, EdgeDepth}), `Edges` (`VeinEdge` {Parent, Child, Length, Yaw}), `Stamps` (`VeinStamp` {Id, Kind, Node, Zone, Edge, X, Y, Yaw, Length, Width, AtlasRow, AtlasColumn, OnWater, Live, RevealAt, Alpha}; a hidden node has no stamps), helpers `DeltaX(a, b)` (the period-wrapped x offset), `ShoreEdges()`, `Trunks(minWidth)`, `Revealed(node, hours)`, `Branches`, `Record()`, `Sha256()`.
- MMR-289 (features): sources are `Sources[i].Node`; trunks are `Trunks(w)` (edges at least w wide at the child); a feature at a node shows once `node.RevealAt <= hours`; `node.Hidden` (a generated node under a live cell) gets none. Implemented: `docs/magic-features.md` (`World/MagicFeatures.cs`, `MagicFeaturesRuntime.Layer` in `Layers`).
- MMR-290 (water): the water stamps are `Stamps` with `OnWater` (water cores have no ground atlas row: `AtlasRow` -1, so MMR-290 picks its own cell); the shore crossings are `ShoreEdges()`. The water stamps share ids and reveal times with the graph, so a cast's water veins grow with its land veins. Implemented: `docs/magic-water.md` (`World/MagicWater.cs`, `MagicWaterRuntime.Instance` in `Layers` after the features). The water layer implements `IMagicWaterVeins` (`DrawsWaterStamps` true in mode `surface`), so the ground plans with the water veins drawn (A' 774, B' 2,012 land stamps instead of 559 / 1,443 without a water layer), and it skips the water ids in `MagicGroundRuntime.Plan.Dropped` (components below `minStamps`). It does not use `VeinStamp.Alpha` (its stamps draw at `magicWater.opacity`).
- `MagicGroundRuntime.Layers` (`IMagicVeinLayer`: `Install(scene, graph, hours, image)`, `Changed(graph, hours)`, `RefreshImages(image)`, `Release(native)`): register a layer once; the runtime calls it at the map's install (after the decals), after each live change and each reveal step (the steps are the vein graph's node reveal times, so a layer's stamps show at their own `RevealAt`; MMR-290), at a seam refresh and at release (`Release(true)` on the live scene, `Release(false)` after `Scene.ClearAll`; `Release(false)` also follows `Release(true)`).

## Colour grade and nodes

- **Grade cells.** Each cell of the 512 x 512 grade grid (every physical image) over a retyped cell takes the grade of the shared table's first land biome with the remapped face type (Desert 20, Plain 40, Forest 60, Steppe 80, Swamp 100, Snow 120), through MMR-256's `GeneratedMapAtmosphere.SetGradeCell`; cleared cells get the world's grade back. A `MapColorGradeManager` is never instantiated. Default taken: generated variants keep the climate's grade (they are visual only, and recolouring a third of the land is outside the look the climate set).
- **Nodes** (MMR-261's `MagicNode`: the capital a LeylineEdit grew): a separate root entity (`GameEntity.CreateEmpty`), never inside a baked settlement, 1.5 units above the ground, with a point light of the node sample's variant colour (radius 8, intensity 40) and a vanilla campaign-map particle system (`psys_fire_smoke_env_point` for fire and infernal, else `psys_mapicon_lightclouds`). Removed natively when a cast drops it; on the map's teardown (after `Scene.ClearAll`) only its handle goes (MMR-262). Defaults taken; MMR-247 judges the look.

## API for MMR-261 (`MagicGroundRuntime`, engine thread)

- `Restore(sceneName, IEnumerable<MutationCell>, IEnumerable<MagicNode>)`: the stored live cells and nodes of a world, from its `SyncData` during a load (before the map loads). The map load builds the snapshot and faces from them. Called while that world's map is up, it applies at once.
- `RestoreDisabled(sceneName)`: RT-37 at load (a digest mismatch): no live cells, casts refused, generated ground drawn.
- `Set(IEnumerable<MutationCell>, IEnumerable<MagicNode>)` (MMR-261): a cast's whole live state during play, at a safe point; it replaces the last state (fwg apply's delta is against the generated world) and updates the stored replay. It throws as `Apply` does, and when mutations are disabled.
- `Apply(IEnumerable<MutationCell>, IEnumerable<MagicNode>)`: a cast's changed cells during play, at a safe point. It swaps the snapshot, rebuilds the face types, re-chooses the changed battle cells in every image, redraws the changed decals and grade cells, adds the nodes and returns the `MagicChange` (with the land shares before and after). It throws when no generated map is loaded, the world is magic-off or mutations are disabled; the caller refuses the cast then.
- `DisableMutations(reason)`: RT-37 during play.
- `Snapshot`: the live `MagicSnapshot` (`LiveCells`, `Nodes`, `RetypedCells`, `CellTypes`, `Digest`, `CanonicalText()`); `LastChange`.
- `MutationCell { Row, Column, Variant, Potency }`: a source sample (row from the north pole line, column 0..n-1), its new `biome_variant` (-1 clears it) and the school's potency (NaN: full). This is the shape of `fwg apply`'s changed cells.
- `MagicNode { School, X, Y }` (canonical physical).

## Live mutation (MMR-261)

A campaign's magic events change the ground around a capital mid-campaign, as FWGC++ computes it (owner decisions 2 and 7; findings section 7; RT-37). The model is `World/WorldMutations.cs` (pure managed, tested offline); the engine side is `Campaign/WorldMutationBehavior.cs`. The policy is `worldgen/policies/mutation.json` (module-embedded, not a compiler input).

**Trigger.**
- `WorldMutations.Report(MagicEvent { school, settlement, strength })` (no magic system yet: owner decision 7), and the debug console: `mnbl_magic.cast <school> <settlement id or name> [strength]` (strength defaults to `castStrength`, 10, and the batch runs at once) and `mnbl_magic.list` (producer, edits, pending, residue, last error).
- Only the eight visible schools are cast; hidden schools, unknown settlements and non-positive strengths are refused.
- The daily batch adds each event's strength to its (school, settlement) residue. A residue at or above `residueThreshold` (10) emits one `LeylineEdit` and drops by the threshold.

**The capital rule** (`MutationSites`, MMR-279's `kingdoms[].memberRealms`):
- the event settlement's realm: a town's own (its source uid in `heroes.realms[].city_uids`), a castle's garrison town's, a village's bound settlement's (default taken: FWGC++ has no realm for castles and villages);
- the edit goes to that realm's FWGC++ capital (`capital_uid`) while the campaign kingdom the realm was merged into still holds it; else to that kingdom's ruling house's home; else (no kingdom left) to the event settlement (default taken).
- A city site plants its key point by `city_uid`; any other site by the direction of its source sample (row z from latitude +90, column x from longitude -180, on FWGC++'s sphere; the test checks it against the direction FWGC++ resolves for Nerdun).
- The first edit at a site plants a key point (`mnbl-cast-<school>-<settlement>`) of `firstIntensity` (4: lower intensities rarely change a variant); each later one raises it by `intensityStep` up to `maxIntensity`. At most `maxNodes` (128, FWGC++'s cap per shift) key points and `maxPending` (32) pending edits.

**Compute.**
- `fwg apply --request <the bundle's source/request.json> --operations <ops> --delta <delta>` (MMR-250; `producer.lock.json` `commands.apply`). The operations are compacted: one shift with one `new_node` per key point at its current intensity, which FWGC++ replays on the freshly generated world (a node raised twice is the same field as one planted at the higher intensity).
- It runs on a background thread (`MutationRunner`) as an owned child process (`GenerationProbe.RunProducerApply`, role `producer_apply`, timeout `applyTimeoutSeconds`). About 2.7 s and 300 MB per call (MMR-250).
- A failed run, including one that hits the timeout, keeps its edits pending and waits for the next daily batch before it runs again (no relaunch at every tick). Only the world's release or a load beginning cancels a run; a cancel drops its outcome.
- Each run works in its own folder, `mutations/apply-<day>-<edits>-<id>` under the module's store (`SubModule.Store`), removed once its outcome is applied, refused or dropped by a cancel (after the cancelled process has exited).
- **Never during a load:** the runner refuses while `GeneratedWorldLoader.Busy`, while a load's `SyncData` has been read and its load has not finished, and while no map overlay is up; the worker checks again before the launch; `GeneratedWorldLoader.Begin` cancels a running job; the launcher itself refuses during a load. The loader's audit (`Report["generatorStarted"]`) counts the `producer_apply` role with the generator's.
- The outcome waits for a safe point: the campaign tick on the map (engine thread). There the delta's cells whose `biome_variant` changed become the whole live state (`MagicGroundRuntime.Set`: the delta is against the generated world, so it replaces the last state), with one node per key point. A delta whose `before` differs from the world's generated layer (another producer build or world) is refused and its edits dropped. The world's release (the game manager's teardown) cancels a running job.

**Persist.** One `SyncData` string, `_mnblWorldMutations`: the canonical `mnbl.world-mutations` v1 document.
- Members, in this order: `schema`, `version`, `producer` (the build the campaign casts with), `world` (world.json's SHA-256), `request` (request.json's), `day`, `edits` (applied), `pending`, `cells` (`[row, column, variant, potency]`), `nodes`, `residue`, `digest`.
- No whitespace; integers plainly; doubles `G17` and floats `G9` (both round-trip), NaN as null; the digest is the SHA-256 of the document without its digest member. A document parses and writes back byte for byte.
- A load replays the stored cells and nodes only (`MagicGroundRuntime.Restore`, before the map loads); pending edits run at the first safe point after the load.
- **RT-37:** a document whose digest does not verify, or one of another world or request, goes to `RestoreDisabled`: no live cells, every cast refused, the generated magic ground still drawn, and the document saved back unchanged (the log is kept).
- The ledger's entry 3 (`worldgen/contracts/save-type-ledger.json`), save ids v2, and the header key `mnbl.campaign.mutationProducer`.

**Producer updates (RT-37).** The campaign casts with the build that generated its world (the bundle's `source.producer.binarySha256`), recorded in its document. The package keeps every build a campaign may cast with at `Producers/<sha256>/fwg.exe` (`worldgen/producer-archive.json`; Git LFS). A cast is refused only when that build is not listed, is missing or has other bytes; stored mutations always load. A re-pin adds the new build to the archive (`tests/check_producer_lock.py` requires the pinned build there) and removes none.

**No decay in v1.**

**Offline numbers** (A' seed 42, a fire cast of intensity 4 at Nerdun; `evidence/world/world-mutation.json`, `tools/Test-WorldMutation.ps1`): the delta lists 375 cells, 29 with a new `biome_variant` (the cells equal MMR-250's recorded delta); 29 live cells retype 7 travel cells within `landValid` (Desert 0.2624 -> 0.2661, Steppe 0.4594 -> 0.4557); the document is about 1.5 KB; the real `fwg apply` on the runner took about 2.7 s.
